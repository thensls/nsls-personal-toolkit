# Harvest asks less and shows what it left out

## Why

A test on a real 20-minute leadership call proposed 13 items. Several were plumbing between the
people in the room, interim rollout steps or non-news, and two described the same fix. The
items left out were hidden, so there was no way to tell the filter was working.

## What Changed

- **A shorter list.** A second pass merges items about the same thing, updates an existing entry
  instead of adding a new one, and drops plumbing, interim steps and non-news. The test: would
  someone outside the meeting look this up in three weeks?
- **Fewer questions.** Minor or unreleased items are decided for you, not asked.
- **Everything starts ticked.** Say `yes` to keep them all, or `untick 3 5` for the ones you don't want.
- **You see what was left out**, one line each, on your own screen only. It is never logged or saved.
