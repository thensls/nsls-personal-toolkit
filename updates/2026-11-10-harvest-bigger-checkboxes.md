# Harvest's review list shows clear checkboxes

## Why

The small tick boxes on the harvest review list looked like decoration, so it wasn't obvious you
could change which items go in.

## What Changed

- **Green ✅ for items going in, ⬜ for items left out**, instead of the small text boxes.
- **Each heading says how to change it**: "To take one out, reply untick 3", "To add one, reply
  yes + u1".

## Cost to Adopt

**1 min.** Arrives on its own at your next session.

## Safe Merge

It changes how one skill shows its review list; your own edits to the skill are kept.

## Opt-Out Guide

- Say "turn off KB harvest" to stop the daily harvest.
