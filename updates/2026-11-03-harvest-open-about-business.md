# Harvest keeps business facts, protects people

## Why

Harvest was holding back exactly the facts a company knowledge base is for: deal sizes, partner
pricing, vendor terms, security gaps and open legal questions. It now keeps those and stays
careful only about people.

## What Changed

- **Kept now:** partner and deal details, vendor prices and terms, security gaps and who is fixing
  them, open legal and compliance questions, what partners and competitors are doing.
- **Still never added:** anyone's pay, people decisions about a named person, private life,
  profit and cash, anything not yet announced, passwords or access secrets, student data.
- **Being named is fine.** If a name is tied to fault ("slipped because Sam missed the deadline"),
  harvest asks you instead of cutting it.
- **Equity and comp program terms** are trimmed to "the program exists".

## Cost to Adopt

**2 min.** Say **"update personal productivity"**.

## Safe Merge

It changes one skill's reference files; your own edits to them are kept.
