# Claude's coaching shows up in your Command Center

## Why

Open-day and close-day already work out coaching for you, but it stayed in the
chat or in one line of the closed-day card, so most of it was never read.

## What Changed

- **Close-day writes two to four lines of coaching** for you each evening: what
  went well, a pattern worth noticing, tomorrow's one focus, and any coaching
  move for someone you'll see. It opens on the closed-day screen.
- **The closed day asks about tomorrow straight away.** "Open tomorrow →" takes
  you to tomorrow's Plan your day, already filled with last night's suggestions.
- **Tomorrow morning the coaching is waiting**, collapsed at the top of Plan
  your day and the Command Center. Open-day adds the day's coaching to it.
- **If Claude is still writing it,** the section says so and fills in on its own
  while you plan.

## Cost to Adopt

**0 min.** Arrives on its own with the toolkit's next update.

## Safe Merge

Only the coaching instructions in `skills/open-day/SKILL.md` (Step 6) and
`skills/close-day/SKILL.md` (the note template and Step 8) change.
